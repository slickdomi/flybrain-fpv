What is real

What the fly does: every neuron and synapse is the real fly's. It sees the world through its own eyes and decides which way to turn and climb.

What the game does: everything else. The drone and its speed, how the fly's firing rates turn into flight, the escape hop, the target marker and dive assist, and the world: trucks, road, trees, bomb, respawns. The fly sees the trucks as grey boxes, the target in black. It never sees explosions or wrecks, and its brain keeps running while a drone is down.
